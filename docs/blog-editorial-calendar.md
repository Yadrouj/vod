## Source and freshness policy

Institutional references include IMDb Help, studio pages, BFI, the Academy, Cannes, La Biennale and artist-controlled pages. Sources are visible in each article. No copied full reviews, lyrics, invented interviews or fictional autobiographies. Original illustrations are labeled, not represented as documentary festival or celebrity photographs.

Current catalog changes and original release dates are separate facts. Related cards are assembled just before publication. Episode cards carry the discovered season/episode. Old IMDb snapshots are not promoted to current trends; if direct IMDb access is denied, the guide stays useful without claiming a new ranking. A published article's date is not changed merely to make it look fresh.

## Next-month research checklist

Choose genuinely new topics from archive changes and verified official announcements, rather than regenerating this list with synonyms. Pair release reports with useful context: audience fit, previous season, cast history from a cited biography, or an accessible listening route. Assign one primary intent per article and link to the relevant existing topic hub. Keep news dates explicit; distinguish announced, premiered and available in the archive. Recheck sources before publication and document corrections openly.
